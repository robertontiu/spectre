# Session 009 journal (Spectre). Brief: POP ROCK, subject: BECOMING A PARENT (a lively carousel of emotions)

## 1. SIT WITH IT
The feeling I choose: **all of it at once, and you stay on the ride.** Not "a baby is sweet". The first weeks: your pulse is a little too fast the whole time, and every few minutes a different feeling comes round and looks you in the face, and before you've finished feeling it the next one is already arriving, and under all of them there is one thing that doesn't change, a centre you didn't have before. In the body: a quick light heartbeat high in the chest (nerves), then the breath stopping (awe), then a laugh that surprises you, heavy eyelids with the heart still racing, a tenderness that tightens the throat until it is nearly fear. In time: fast and bright, but turning rather than marching: something keeps coming round in threes while the pulse stays in four. In space: a small lit room at night with a huge sky over it; close and enormous at once.
**The ride itself:** a bright clean guitar figure in sixteenths grouped in threes (3+3+3+3+2+2 across each bar) with the accent on the first of each three, so a dotted-eighth lilt keeps turning against a steady four-on-the-bar pulse from kick and bass. That figure is the carousel: it is there in the first bar and the last, it never stops, and the hook is cut from the same cloth (three notes on dotted eighths: "here-you-are", the title). The kick and bass are the pole in the middle.
**The horses, in the order they come round:** (1) NERVES: intro and verse 1, the figure tight and muted, bass holding its breath on a pedal A, a chattering low voice in short phrases. (2) AWE: the pre-chorus, the band stops playing time: ringing chords, one long rising note, the ride bell. (3) JOY: the chorus, wide open, the hook leaping. (4) GRIEF FOR THE OLD LIFE: only a four-bar tag after chorus 1, a lone guitar line over F#m, C#m and a borrowed G (the flat seven, the door you came through), then the ride turns on. One horse, one pass. (5) THE SLEEPLESS BLUR: verse 2a, same song seen through 4 a.m.: tremolo on the figure, a snare that nods off and misses a backbeat, the voice sliding between notes. (6) LAUGHTER: verse 2b, waking into the absurdity: a hiccuping high piano answering the voice. (7) TENDERNESS SO BIG IT'S ALMOST FEAR: the bridge, the band gone, the figure slowed to a music box over D then D minor (the minor iv is the fear inside it), a heartbeat kick, the voice close. (8) PRIDE: the last chorus, a lead guitar singing with the voice, the top note held. (9) LOVE: the outro, where the horses pass again two bars each over the full band and overlap; it ends at home, on A, with the figure still turning alone and stopping mid-turn, because the ride goes on.
**Nearest wrong feelings and what separates them:** (a) *Greeting-card / baby-advert sweetness*: no glockenspiel-and-ukulele brightness, no constant major smile; the nerves are a real fast pulse, the bridge has a real minor iv and a bare heartbeat, the blur is genuinely woozy, and the music box appears once, dry, over the fear chord, not as decoration. (b) *Lullaby / soft ballad*: 136 bpm, a played kit with a real backbeat, the sixteenth figure never slows except once in the bridge, and even there the kick keeps the pulse. (c) *Chaos*: every feeling arrives over the same pulse, key and figure; sections are 4 or 8 bars, one horse at a time until the outro, where the overlaps are planned, not piled. (d) *Melancholy taking over*: grief gets four bars and one borrowed chord; the bridge is tenderness, not sadness, and it is followed by the biggest joy. (e) Not "Anyway" again: that one was a slow climb out of a minor verse at 122, playing behind the beat, adding company step by step, ending on IV. This one is fast, major from bar one, on top of the beat, chorus early, a different colour per section instead of one long build, and it ends on I.
Decisions: **136 bpm**, straight time, **A major**. Verse: A(add9) | F#m7 | D(add9) | Esus4-E (the oldest love-song progression there is, on a pedal A at first). Pre: Bm7 | A/C# | D | Esus4-E (bass climbing B, C#, D, E). Chorus: D | A | E | F#m | D | A | E | E (starts on IV, so it opens like a door; hands back to A). Bridge: D | Dm | A | A, D | Dm | Esus4 | E. The sounds that hold it: the turning clean-guitar figure; jangly open-chord crunch guitars (brighter and less driven than "Anyway") with a tambourine in the chorus; the `vox` hook doubled by a lead guitar late. Working title: "Here You Are".
