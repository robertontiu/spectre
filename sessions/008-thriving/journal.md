# Session 008 journal (Spectre). Brief: POP ROCK, subject: THRIVING OVER DEPRESSION

## 1. SIT WITH IT
The feeling I choose: **winning the days anyway**. Not the first morning, not the look back from safety: the middle of it, living alongside the weight and getting up regardless, and finding that the days you win start to add up into a life with momentum and warmth in it. In the body: legs that are heavy for the first steps and then find a stride; a chest that was held shut opening a notch at a time; the moment you realise you are walking at the pace of the people next to you again. In time: it starts slow-feeling inside a steady pulse (the clock never stopped, you just couldn't move with it), and the song is about catching up with that pulse until you are pushing it. In space: first a small close room, one guitar and your own breath; then a door; then the whole band in a big room around you, people, not a stadium.
Nearest wrong feelings and what separates them: (a) **toxic positivity / advert anthem**: no stomp-clap, no "whoa-oh" gang chant, no key change, not bright from bar 1; the verse must be genuinely heavy (minor vi home, low narrow voice, dark clean guitar, almost nothing else) so the light has something to stand on. (b) **Wallowing (sad song with a loud chorus)**: the chorus must move forward, not just get loud: 8th-note drive in bass and guitars, a melody that reaches up and LANDS, a major centre; the verses get more motion as the song goes, the second verse already walks. (c) **Teen-angst rage**: no pop-punk tempo, no distortion all the time, no palm-muted fury; drive is warm crunch, the loudest guitars only in choruses. (d) **Clean victory / "cured"**: the last chorus doesn't end on a big I with a rock ending; the dark verse figure comes back inside the final chorus, and the song ends on IV (an unresolved Asus2), still moving: tomorrow is another day to win.
The arc: the clean figure alone (the room), a heartbeat kick, verse 1 inside the weight; pre-chorus leaning (stepwise bass up); chorus 1 opens, but not all the way; verse 2 walks (drums already in, ride, bass moving) but the weight is still in the harmony; chorus 2 fuller; then the bridge is a bad day: half-time, the band falls away, the verse figure alone in minor, the relapse is real; the drums come back one piece at a time like a heartbeat returning; a beat of silence; the last chorus doubled with a new singing guitar line AND the verse figure inside it; the outro keeps walking and stops on IV.
Decisions: **122 bpm**, straight 8ths, 4/4, played kit. **E major with C# minor as the verse's home** (relative minor = the weight inside the same key). Verse: C#m | A(add9) | E | Bsus4 B (the home is the minor chord). Pre-chorus: A | B | C#m | B (bass climbing A-B-C#, leaning). Chorus: E | B/D# | C#m7 | A, then E | B | Asus2 | A (the chorus never lands back on E at its end: it hands over to IV). The sounds that hold it: (1) a dark, chorused clean-guitar arpeggio on C#m that IS the weight and keeps coming back; (2) the `vox` lead, low "oo" in the verses opening to "ah" in the chorus, the voice that climbs; (3) the double-tracked crunch guitars and a driving bass in 8ths as the forward motion. Working title: "Anyway".
