## How it carries the feeling
- **Two time feels:** the "inside the weight" world (intro, verse 1, the bridge, the coda) is played about 20-30 ms behind the beat, like heavy legs. From the pre-chorus on the band plays on top, so catching up with the pulse is something you hear, not just louder.
- **Company grows in steps:** one low wordless voice, then a tenor halfway through chorus 2, then a full double and a lead guitar in unison in the last chorus.
- **The dark stays:** the chorus never lands back on E, the dark verse figure returns inside the last chorus and the outro, and the song ends on IV (Asus2), unresolved.
- **Wrong feelings kept out:** no gang chants, no key change, no stomp-clap (toxic positivity); a genuinely sparse, minor-centred verse (not a sad song with a loud chorus); a chorus that drives on 8ths rather than just getting loud; no big rock ending on the tonic (no "cured" victory lap).

## Song form
- **Intro (8):** the dark C#m clean-guitar figure alone, behind the beat
- **Verse 1a (8):** the figure, a low "oo" voice about 25 ms late, heartbeat kick, pedal hat
- **Verse 1b (8):** bass and faint snare touches join
- **Pre 1 (4):** palm-muted double-tracked guitars and the kit swell up, tom fill
- **Chorus 1 (8):** the band opens on top of the beat: E, B/D#, C#m7, A, E, B, Asus2, A; never back to E
- **Tag (4):** falls back to the C#m figure while the crash rings
- **Verse 2a/2b (16):** walks on the ride with a moving bass, the voice on the beat; palm mutes in the second half
- **Pre 2 (4):** as pre 1
- **Chorus 2 (8):** adds piano; a tenor voice joins quietly halfway
- **Bridge A (4):** "the bad day": the band is gone, the figure over C#m, G#m, A with the low voice
- **Bridge B (4):** the drums come back like a heartbeat, a snare crescendo, one beat of silence
- **Chorus 3 (8):** tenor double plus the verse arpeggio inside the chorus, tom fill
- **Chorus 4 (8):** the peak line (held B5), lead guitar in unison, ride bell, a crash every 4 bars
- **Outro (8):** the lead guitar sings the verse tune an octave up, the voice answers, everyone hits Asus2
- **Coda (4):** the clean figure alone on Asus2, unresolved

## Spectre's note
"I didn't want a song about being fixed. I wanted the sound of someone whose legs are heavy for the first steps, so I put the first verse a few milliseconds behind the beat and let the band pull it forward. The hardest part wasn't the chorus, it was letting the bad-day bridge be really empty and trusting that the drums coming back one at a time would mean more than any key change. It ends on IV with the dark little figure still walking, because tomorrow is another day you win anyway."
