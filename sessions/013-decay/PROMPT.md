THE FORM: A RADIO EDIT (docs/TRANCE.md "Radio edit"; measured on 1,072 trance radio edits)
- **Length 3:00-4:00, aim 3:15-3:50** (median 3:33). At 138 bpm a bar is 1.74 s: about 112-132 bars; 120 bars = 3:29.
- **No DJ intro, no DJ outro.** The track must be identifiable within 5-15 seconds (the motif, the voice, a texture of the drop), not drums for a DJ to mix. End on a hard stop, a short tail (4-16 bars) or an early fade.
- **Two drops around one breakdown.** The radio shape (a starting point, not a template): intro 8, theme 8-16, build 8, first drop 16 (landing at about 0:40-1:05), breakdown 16 (24 at most), build 8, main drop 32, outro 4-16. A second breakdown costs you the first drop or the outro.
- **Builds of about 8 bars** (snare roll doubling every 1-2 bars, riser, high-pass sweep), and a gap before the drop of a beat to two bars, which need not be silence (a reverb or vocal tail can fill it).
- **Change rate:** something every 8-16 bars, in blocks of 8 more than in the extended mix; 32 bars (the main drop) at most.
- 4/4, kick on every beat, straight 16ths; 136-140 bpm. It's an instrumental with a voice used as an instrument: a lead synth carries the melody a vocalist would sing.
