Context to weigh (docs/ROMANIAN.md, "Anthem"): the anthem was born in the 1848 revolution and has been sung against every regime since; under Austro-Hungarian censorship a choir sang it without words; in February 2017, against an ordinance that partly decriminalised abuse of office, 150,000 people in Piața Victoriei stood in silence and then sang it together; after the Colectiv fire in 2015 people marched under "Corupția ucide" ("corruption kills"). In Romania the anthem is something people sing AT corruption. Its melody is minor and was called plaintive from the start: the beauty in it is already aching.

NEAREST WRONG FEELINGS (name yours in the first journal entry, and what separates each from the target):
- **Cheap shock:** distorting the anthem because distortion is loud and edgy. The damage must mean something (rot spreading, a note bought off, a phrase that can no longer finish), have a cause in the track and grow; a crushed anthem in bar one is a gimmick.
- **Mocking the anthem:** anything that reads as parody: comic detuning, a sloppy or silly quote, the melody under taunting sounds, a "wrong" version played for laughs. The clean quote must be correct and beautiful; the listener must feel the loss when it breaks.
- **Generic rage (002 "Lid Off"):** anger without an object, all heat and speed. This anger has an object (corruption) and something it loves (the country). Rage burns the whole house; this track must keep the house standing and go after the rot.
- **Nationalist pride:** the anthem as a triumphant march, brass and flag-waving, a victory lap, "us against them". No triumph and no enemy people; the enemy is a system and a habit.
- **A protest cliché:** megaphones, sirens, crowd chants, marching drums, a "rise up" build. The protest context is background, not props.
- **Despair:** the country as already dead. Corruption damages and keeps damaging; the beauty is still there, still worth the anger. Don't end on the country's death, and don't end on a victory either: it isn't over.

If something in the doc looks wrong or you need a part it doesn't cover, don't guess: write it in the journal and end the session with a final reply that starts "BLOCKED: anthem melody unverified", so the orchestrator can check it. - **The melody and the words are public domain; later arrangements are not.** Use the bare melody and write your own harmony.
- **Romanian rules (Law 75/1994 and its norms, HG 1157/2001)** fine intoning the anthem "with a text or score other than the one set by law" and ask citizens to commit no act that offends it. A track quoting the melody is not an intonation of the anthem, but stay inside the spirit: quote fragments, never a full performance of the anthem; any clean quote follows the score exactly; never put new words on the anthem's melody, and never sing the anthem's own words over an altered melody; the damage reads as corruption's work on the country, never as mockery. The melody is credited to Anton Pann in the README.
- **What can be damaged, and how, is your decision** (examples, not instructions): the instrument playing it decays (bitcrush, sample-rate reduction, a tape that sags); one note in a phrase is bent or bought off and stays wrong; the phrase stops before it can finish; a dissonant cell gets into the harmony under it and multiplies; the quote is played back through ever worse "copies". The clean quote can return, but it carries what happened to it.

STUDIO NOTES FOR AGGRESSIVE HIP HOP (details in docs/MANUAL.md):
- Drums: `kick`, `snare`, `rim`, `hat`, `openhat`, `tom`, `crash`; for claps use **`handclap`** (its `spread_ms` is in milliseconds; default 9) and **never the old `clap`**, whose `spread` is in seconds: earlier producers set it like a 0-1 width and every clap fired a second hit off the beat. Set the handclap by its peaks, about 6-10 dB under the kick's.
- Low end: `808` (drive for aggression, `tone` for the 2nd harmonic so it speaks on small speakers, glide for slides) or `bass`. Keep the kick and 808 apart (002: kick at 62 Hz, 808 notched and sidechained); vary the 808's notes so the low end is even (005). The measured hip-hop curve has much less sub than the old `beat` curve: 001-005 sat 7-10 dB over it at 40 Hz and the master cut there at its limit. Get weight from saturation and mids, not sub.
- Melody and colour: `strings`, `choir`, `organ`, `piano`, `ep`, `bell`, `musicbox`, `pad`, `lead`; Romanian colour if you want it: `fiddle`, `cimbalom`, `nai`, `oud` (as cobza), `ney` low (caval). Damage: fx `bitcrush`, `srr`, `lofi`, `drive`, `sat`, `pitch`, `lpf`; textures `vinyl`, `tape`; section `exit` `tapestop` and `silence` (use `silence`, not `cut`, for true silence: `cut` doesn't stop tails).
- Top end and phone: distorted snares and claps put noise at 4-12 kHz that reads as static on a phone (002's snare hits were 95% of it). Drive the body, shelve the top; keep vinyl, tape and any noise under about 4-6 kHz. Run `listen --phone --genre hiphop` before mastering and solo any section it flags bright or hissy.
- Master with `studio.py master vNNN --genre hiphop` (measured hip-hop curve, -10.5 LUFS, -1 dBTP). Read the report: no warnings, `eq_at_limit` ideally empty, `top_end_max_abs_dev_db` about 1.5 or less, the phone check without notes. If the EQ cuts hard, fix the mix.
