## How it carries the feeling
- **Reunion is one verse broken across a door.** One voice alone outside sings the question, 35 ms behind the beat; the house comes in under it as a muffled organ, and the last line is answered by a second voice from inside.
- **The door is a key change and a change of space.** The A♭ chord holds while one inner note rises a semitone (A♭ becomes F7), the filter opens, and the song lands in B♭, on the beat, in the room.
- **The meeting is in the voice-leading.** The quoted phrase, "Peace on the earth, good will to men", falls an octave; one voice holds D4 and the other falls from D5 onto it. The one who stayed holds a note, the one who left falls onto it. The organ lets go under the unison: two voices, one pitch.
- **After the meeting, the house fills.** Three handbells ring a plain hunt; verse 2 passes between the parlour piano and three voices; verse 3 gathers five voices, organ, pedal, cello and bells. The quote returns once, a cappella and quieter, before the full ending, and the street bell has the last two notes.
- **Wrong feelings kept out:** greeting-card kitsch (no sleigh bells, no glockenspiel sparkle), a shopping-mall jingle, a sad ballad (the years are a shadow inside the warmth, not the subject), a church service (only one sung line of the carol, no sermon), a novelty song.

## Song form (carol bars of 6/8)
- **Bell (about 3 bars, A♭):** one handbell rings the tune's home phrase; the caller breathes in
- **Verse 1, the street (8):** one voice alone, then the house muffled under line 3, the answer from inside on line 4
- **Door (1⅓):** the chord holds, one inner note rises, the filter opens
- **Verse 1, the meeting (8, B♭):** the quote on two voices meeting in unison, then Spectre's own rising line and the home lines, the pedal entering
- **Interlude 1 (4):** three handbells ring a plain hunt over a walking bass
- **Verse 2 (16):** parlour piano over organ, three voices answering; the voices hum the held D while the bells carry the pulse
- **Interlude 2 (4):** the opening bell phrase again, now in B♭ with organ
- **Verse 3 (16 + hold):** five voices, organ, pedal, cello, bells; the quote returns a cappella, 4 dB down; a ritardando and a held chord
- **End:** organ and pedal carry the chord and fade under the street bell's last two notes

## Departures from Spectre's habits
The centre of gravity is early and quiet: the meeting falls at about a fifth of the track and is its most exposed moment, not a peak built toward, and there is no valley at two thirds. The key lift happens once, inside verse 1, as the door, not before a last chorus. There is no silence before the arrival: the join is a held chord with one moving inner voice. Spectre's first track with no beat at all.

## Spectre's note
"I started by arranging somebody else's carol and it sounded like a rehearsal. The note from the artist was right, and the piece only became mine when I threw the carol out and kept one line of it. That line already had the reunion inside it: a tune that drops an octave onto a note someone else can hold. So I wrote a door around it. Most of the work after that was taking things away from the moment they meet until it was two voices and one pitch."
