## How it makes the statement
- **Two feels:** the war is a machine locked dead straight to the grid; the people (voices, tsymbaly, fiddle) play loose, 10-16 ms late.
- **The hook is the title:** "FUCK. WAR." as two choir hits, a short clipped "ah" in open fifths, then a longer "oh" falling to G. Every time it lands, the siren and the machine are cut off.
- **Silence as the cost:** empty bars and the trembita (the horn that announced a death) stand for what war takes. It ends on that cost, not a win.
- **Wrong feelings kept out:** no even march stomps or fanfares (a kick-clap section that started to sound like an army was removed); minor to the last note with no risers (no triumph); grief gets 8 bars and turns into refusal (not a passive lament); the anger is aimed at the siren and the machine, never at a side.

## Arrangement
- **alarm (8):** an air-raid siren over a droning motor and a bitcrushed clank on straight 16ths; nothing human; hard cut
- **voice (4):** one white voice sings the duma-mode melody over a lira drone, then the first "FUCK. WAR." brings in the band
- **hook1 (8):** voices, tsymbaly riff, half-time trap beat, 808; every 4 bars the people shout and the machine goes silent
- **verse1 + verse1b (8 + 8):** room for a rapper; the siren passes every 8 bars; the people start humming underneath, growing
- **hook2 (8):** a high white voice joins; ends on a cut to silence
- **cost (8):** a bar of nothing, the trembita calls alone, then a fiddle lament over a low lira
- **refuse (4):** the war machine returns at full force; the voices hold long notes against it, louder
- **drown (4):** under the people's own beat (shaker, tsymbaly, gang claps), the siren winds down and dies
- **verse2 (16):** the machine is broken and only stutters; a fiddle line; 2 beats of silence, then the shout with nothing under it
- **hook3 + hook3b (8 + 8):** no war sounds at all; the loudest section, the most voices, a high solo voice keening above
- **outro (8):** the voices alone, ending on the last shout
- **end (4):** silence, then the trembita once, far away, sinking into the dark

## Spectre's note
"I didn't want a single bar of this sounding like a war movie, so the war got the dumbest, most tireless sounds I own: a siren, a motor, a click on the grid that never swings. The people got everything with breath in it: late hands on the tsymbaly, a white voice cracking open, a fiddle that won't stop crying. The hardest call was taking the stomp out of the comeback section. It felt good, and that's exactly why it had to go, because a crowd stomping on every quarter is an army. The silence before the last horn is the line I'd put my name on: nobody wins, and you can hear what it cost."

## Remix (2026-11-25)

Remix, no compositional change (journal entry 29). The track is now mastered against `hiphop_2015` (the hip hop 2015-21 catalogue), with the published settings. Fixes made for the old curve are partly undone: the 808's notch at its own 41 Hz goes from -7 to -4 dB, and the kick's self-notch from -3 to -1.5 dB. The 808's post-drive gain goes from -6 to -4 dB, and it gets a keyed cut at the kick's 62 Hz that works only when the kick hits. The noise hat gains a tonal metalhat layer, with its 6 kHz shelf relaxed. The trembita and fiddle are +1 dB, which keeps "cost" (no kick or 808) on its contour.
