# Modern-strand tools: A/B and demos

Built 2026-11-25 for the next trance session (standing rule 19: never start a session without what the producer needs), from the gaps in docs/TRANCE.md "Modern trance production" G and I. Each case is a short project (138 bpm, 8 bars, about 14 s) rendered twice: **A** = what the engine could do before, **B** = the new tool; both through the engine's quick master at -10.5 LUFS (loudness-matched, no tonal EQ), 192 kbps MP3. Pictures: mel spectrograms, A left, B right. Numbers: [numbers.json](numbers.json). Parameters and measurements: docs/MANUAL.md "Modern strands".

| Case | A | B | Picture | What to listen for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shimmer reverb | [shimmer-A.mp3](shimmer-A.mp3) | [shimmer-B.mp3](shimmer-B.mp3) | [shimmer.png](shimmer.png) | A pluck arp for 4 bars, then its tail. A: a long hall. B: a `shimmer` return (+12, decay 7 s, amount 0.7): the tail climbs an octave and keeps ringing. Tail (bars 6-8) against the playing part: A -33.4 dB, B -23.2 dB; 4-16 kHz against 1-4 kHz in the tail: A -16.0 dB, B -5.8 dB. |
| Granular / freeze | [granular-A.mp3](granular-A.mp3) | [granular-B.mp3](granular-B.mp3) | [granular.png](granular.png) | The same pluck. B: a `granular` return, stretched to half speed while the pluck plays, then frozen (`scan` 0) on the moment before the pluck stopped, for 4 bars, density rising 40 to 90: the pluck turned into a pad. Tail against the playing part: A -33.7 dB, B +0.5 dB. The frozen moment holds the pluck's attacks, heard as a soft grain texture. |
| Acid | [acid-A.mp3](acid-A.mp3) | [acid-B.mp3](acid-B.mp3) | [acid.png](acid.png) | A 2-bar 16th line with accents (`!`) and slides (`~`) over a kick. A: the old approximation (polysynth, resonant filter, envelope, drive): accents only louder, no slides. B: `acid`, cutoff 180 to 700 Hz and decay 0.25 to 0.7 s automated over the 8 bars (the "knob ride"). Accents and slides measured in docs/MANUAL.md "Acid voice". |
| 909 drums | [909-A.mp3](909-A.mp3) | [909-B.mp3](909-B.mp3) | [909.png](909.png), [909-vs-reference.png](909-vs-reference.png) | Clubkick in both; A: `handclap`, `snare`, `kitride`; B: `clap909`, `snare909`, `ride909`, same patterns. The fit to a recorded TR-909: [measure909.py](measure909.py) (table in docs/MANUAL.md "909 drums"; spectrum within 2.0 / 3.2 / 2.9 dB RMS for snare / clap / ride, against 4.5-14.5 dB for the earlier sounds). |
| Slide and accent on the polysynth | [slide-A.mp3](slide-A.mp3) | [slide-B.mp3](slide-B.mp3) | [slide.png](slide.png) | A lead line with `~` and `!`. A: they change nothing but velocity. B: `glide_ms` 90 and `accent` 0.9: slides into the tied notes, accented notes open the filter further. |
| Automated sound | [automation-A.mp3](automation-A.mp3) | [automation-B.mp3](automation-B.mp3) | [automation.png](automation.png) | A supersaw pad. A: every chord the same. B: section `inst` automation over the 8 bars: detune 8 to 34 cents, drift 0 to 0.8, cutoff LFO depth 0.02 to 0.9 octave and rate 0.3 to 3 Hz, cutoff 1.5 to 5 kHz: the pad grows from still to alive. Spectral centroid bars 1-2 / 7-8: A 754 / 747 Hz, B 645 / 886 Hz; the values each chord was rendered with are in numbers.json (16 chords, detune 8.0 ... 32.4, LFO rate 0.30 ... 2.60 Hz). |
| Resonance suppressor | [suppress-A.mp3](suppress-A.mp3) | [suppress-B.mp3](suppress-B.mp3) | [suppress.png](suppress.png) | A resonant saw lead driven hard (drive 14 dB, sat 0.4). B: `suppress` after the saturation (depth 8, threshold 2): the whistling band is lower, the note stays. Largest bulge of the spectrum's peak envelope over its 1-octave trend (0.5-12 kHz): A 8.1 dB, B 6.3 dB (on the test signal in docs/MANUAL.md: 10.0 to 5.2 dB). |

Rebuild: `bash tools/remix/waitfree.sh && python3 examples/production/modern/build.py [case]` (about 25 s for all). The 909 measurement downloads the reference kit to `~/.cache/spectre/refs/tr909` (measurement only; delete it after): `python3 examples/production/modern/measure909.py`.

The engine's test project for all of this: `examples/trance-modern-test.json` (acid with automated cutoff, 909 drums, a shimmer return whose amount rises through the break, a granular return frozen in the last section, `suppress` on the acid, polysynth slide and accent, LFO and detune automation).
